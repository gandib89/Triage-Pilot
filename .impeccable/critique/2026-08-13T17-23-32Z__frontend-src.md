## Overall Impression

The visual craft sits well above the score. What drags it down is the interaction contract around the product's one irreversible action: sending an AI-drafted reply to a real customer costs one unguarded click, while deleting a closed ticket gets a browser dialog. The severity ordering is inverted.

## What's Working

1. **The token layer is real.** index.css:21-25 documents why status colors are darkened ("so they clear 4.5:1 as text on #FFFFFF"), and zero hardcoded color in components backs that up.
2. **Panel concentric radii are exact.** ui.jsx:12-20 has an outer 2rem tray, p-1.5, inner 1.625rem core. 2rem minus 0.375rem. The math was done.
3. **prefers-reduced-motion honored at all 5 motion sites**, zero misses. Rare at this level of motion ambition.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Approve/Reject irreversible, unconfirmed, unexplained
**What:** DecisionDetail.jsx:193-206 renders three equal-weight buttons that send AI-drafted text to a real customer. No confirm, no undo, no copy stating what Reject does downstream.
**Why it matters:** The landing page claims "nothing reaches a customer without you." One frictionless click makes the human a rubber stamp, the exact failure mode human-in-the-loop exists to prevent.
**Fix:** Approve/Reject expand in place to "Send this reply to {customer}?" with the first 120 chars of outgoing text. Add a 6-8s undo before the POST commits. Add one line under Reject explaining the customer-visible effect.
**Suggested command:** /impeccable harden

### [P0] Customer submit dead-ends on a staff-only route
**What:** NewTicket.jsx:35 navigates to /triage/:id; ProtectedRoute.jsx:65-67 bounces the customer to /tickets with no acknowledgment. Triage failure is silently swallowed by `catch {}` at line 36.
**Why it matters:** It is the end of the primary customer journey, and peak-end weights it hardest. It currently reads as indifference.
**Fix:** Navigate to /tickets/:ticketId with state:{justCreated:true} and render a confirmation plus the triage outcome. Surface "Submitted, but triage hasn't run yet" instead of nothing.
**Suggested command:** /impeccable harden

### [P1] Zero focus rings on buttons and links
**What:** Detector-confirmed 0 `:focus-visible` and 0 `focus:` variants. Button (ui.jsx:36-58), NavPill, and every raw button and Link are affected. Tailwind preflight suppresses the default.
**Why it matters:** WCAG 2.4.7 failure, and it kills the keyboard workflow before shortcuts are even added.
**Fix:** Add a token-based focus-visible ring to the Button and NavPill primitives and to every raw interactive element.
**Suggested command:** /impeccable audit

### [P1] Raw JSON error payloads shown to users
**What:** JSON.stringify(err.response.data) at NewTicket.jsx:27, DecisionDetail.jsx:56 and :70, MessageThread.jsx:51. A failed approve shows a serialized DRF dict at the moment the operator most needs clarity.
**Why it matters:** It leaks backend field structure and is visually incoherent inside a system this carefully typeset.
**Fix:** Login.jsx:31-37 already has `extractError`. Lift it to api.js, use it at all four sites, delete the three ad-hoc handlers.
**Suggested command:** /impeccable clarify

### [P1] Operate mode inherits Persuade motion and density
**What:** TriageQueue.jsx:69-77 staggers rows at 40ms each, so row 20 lands ~1.4s after row 1, and each row is a bevelled 2rem tray at py-6. PageFade.jsx:8 costs 700ms per navigation. DecisionDetail.jsx:54 sends the operator back through the whole re-animation after every decision.
**Why it matters:** What reads as luxurious once reads as latency on the fortieth pass.
**Fix:** Cap the stagger at 6 rows, drop PageFade to 200ms, and give the queue flat hairline-divided rows at py-3. Target 15+ rows per viewport.
**Suggested command:** /impeccable optimize

## Questions to Consider

1. If the operator approves 95% without editing, is this oversight or a consent ceremony? Nothing costs anything to approve: no confidence score, no confirm, no weight difference between reasoning and payload. What would this screen look like with friction proportional to risk?
2. The landing page sells "three keys" and the app ships zero shortcuts. Which is the lie, the marketing or the belief that these users are power users?
3. Why does deleting a closed ticket get a dialog while sending an AI reply to a @browser:customer gets nothing? That inversion shows which risks got modeled and which did not.
